About Us

About Food Rescue

Food Rescue is a web-based platform developed to reduce food wastage by connecting hotels, restaurants, bakeries, and catering services with customers.

Businesses can upload details of surplus food including quantity, availability time, description, and price. Customers can browse available food and place orders through the platform.

The platform provides a simple way to give surplus food a second chance while helping businesses reduce food waste and allowing customers to access food at affordable prices.
